# iFeel

A calm emotions wheel for checking in with yourself.

- Spin the wheel or drag it with your finger; the slice under the pointer grows as it passes.
- Tap a slice to open it: the wheel blooms into the next level (core → secondary → specific).
- Tap the centre to go back. "That's it" saves the feeling with a strength (1–5) and an optional note.
- The Journal shows the last two weeks as coloured dots, plus your check-ins.

Single-file web app (`index.html`), no build step. Entries stay in the browser's localStorage.
Word list based on the common three-level Feelings Wheel (7 core feelings, 41 secondary, 82 specific).
